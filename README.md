# E-Commerce Sales Analysis - Olist Dataset

## 1. Dashboard Summary & KPIs
* **Total Revenue:** R$ 13.5M+ 
* **Total Orders:** ~99,000+ orders processed
* **Top Product Category:** Health & Beauty (highest total revenue generator)
* **Best Performing Month:** November (driven by seasonal shopping spikes)
* **Top Region:** São Paulo (SP) — leading state in total sales volume

---

## 2. Business Insights Report (5 Numbered Insights)

1. **Revenue Concentration in Core Categories**
   * *Finding:* Categories like Health & Beauty and Bed, Bath & Table drive the highest gross merchandise value (GMV) across the platform. 
   * *Recommendation:* Allocate a larger share of promotional marketing budget and secure exclusive vendor partnerships for these top categories to maximize total revenue.

2. **Seasonal Sales Volatility and Spikes**
   * *Finding:* Sales experience massive spikes during specific months (notably November due to Black Friday shopping events).
   * *Recommendation:* Scale up logistics partners, warehouse inventory, and customer support staff well in advance of Q4 to seamlessly handle high order volumes and prevent fulfillment bottlenecks.

3. **Geographic Sales Concentration**
   * *Finding:* A significant majority of total orders and revenue originate from the state of São Paulo (SP) and the broader Southeast region.
   * *Recommendation:* Optimize regional fulfillment hubs and inventory placement closer to São Paulo to dramatically cut down transit times and reduce average freight costs.

4. **Stable Average Order Value (AOV)**
   * *Finding:* The Average Order Value remains relatively flat month-over-month despite heavy fluctuations in total order volume.
   * *Recommendation:* Introduce targeted cross-selling, product bundling, or free shipping thresholds at checkout to actively encourage customers to increase their cart sizes.

5. **Impact of Delivery Performance on Reviews**
   * *Finding:* While overall customer review scores skew positively (mostly 4 and 5 stars), the majority of low ratings (1-2 stars) correlate directly with delayed delivery dates.
   * *Recommendation:* Prioritize stricter Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with logistics carriers and improve real-time tracking transparency to protect customer retention.

---

## 3. Surprising Finding Note
> **Surprising Trend:** Despite occasional logistical friction and extended shipping times reported in various regions, the vast majority of Olist customers still leave 4- and 5-star ratings. A deeper look reveals that customers are exceptionally forgiving of delivery delays as long as the physical product quality meets or exceeds expectations, meaning product quality acts as a powerful buffer against fulfillment dissatisfaction.
